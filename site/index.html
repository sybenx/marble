What it does

Your account

It's yours alone. No company holds it, and no company can take it away.

Sign in anywhere with your phone

A site shows a code. You scan it with Marble. You're in, with no password.

Discover

Posts from people you'll like, plus today's stories.

Why it's safe

Your account is split between your phone and a few servers. Your phone and one server have to work together to do anything as you. Nobody, not even you, has the whole thing in one place.

  • Lose your phone, keep your account. Get it back on a new phone from your paper backup or another device.
  • A server alone can't act as you. If one goes down or goes bad, your account carries on with the others.
  • Your paper backup is the spare. Print it once and keep it somewhere safe, like your passport.

Privacy

  • The servers that hold part of your account see what you do through them, like a post or a sign-in. Never your messages, and never enough to act as you.
  • When you search for people, what you type goes to the servers Marble searches. When Marble checks an address like name@example.com, that site can see your IP address.
  • Pictures and videos load from wherever they're kept. Those places can see your IP address, as in any app that shows you a picture.
  • This page has no tracking, no cookies and nothing loaded from anywhere else.
