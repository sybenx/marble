About Marble

Why it's safe

Your account is split between your phone and a few servers. Your phone and one server have to work together to do anything as you. Nobody, not even you, has the whole thing in one place.

  • Lose your phone, keep your account. Get it back on a new phone from your paper backup or another device.
  • A server alone can't act as you. If one goes down or goes bad, your account carries on with the others.
  • Your paper backup is the spare. Print it once and keep it somewhere safe, like your passport.

Privacy

  • This site is plain files: everything happens in your browser. It has no tracking, no cookies, and nothing of its own loaded from anywhere else.
  • When you search here, what you type goes only to the servers Marble searches. When you open a post or a profile, the servers that keep it see which one.
  • When Marble checks an address like name@example.com (once you've typed all of it), that site can see your IP address and which address was asked about. A handle like @name.bsky.social is checked with Bluesky. Pictures and videos load from wherever they're kept, and those places can too.
  • In the app, the servers that hold part of your account see what you do through them, like a post or a sign-in. Never your messages, and never enough to act as you.
