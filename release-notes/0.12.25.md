Marble 0.12.25 (beta)

- **A quieter Marble, part one** (the new plan: Search, Notes, and Reader for the sources you choose):
  - Messages is hidden until messages work. It's two panes for now: Following and Search.
  - Posts in Following no longer have reaction, reply and repost buttons. Open a post, or find it in Search, to react or reply.
  - "Beyond your people" is gone from Following: strangers belong in Search.
  - Marble no longer learns topics from what you open and like. You choose your sources.
  - Feed 1 and 2 move to the current Following. Account › Advanced can switch back for this version.
  - Usage notes are turned off.
- **Safer list changes:** following, muting, Special Follow, favourite relays, relay sets and pins each start from your newest list anywhere, change only the one entry, and are checked again before they're signed. If your current list can't be found, Marble refuses rather than starting a new one.
