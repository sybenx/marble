Marble works with no server again. Your key stays on your phone and signs from there; a Keykin box is optional.

- Start with Create your account, I have an account on another device, or I have my secret key.
- Move your key to another device by QRST: the other device shows a code, you scan it here, type its digits, and confirm with your fingerprint.
- Sign in to sites (scanned code, nostrconnect://, bunker://) and let apps on your phone sign (NIP-55), all with the key on your phone.
- Your key is never deleted unless you choose to.
- Optional: Your account, Advanced, Sign in to a Keykin box. Your phone and the box then sign together, and your phone keeps the whole key as your backup.
