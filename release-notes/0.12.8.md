Marble 0.12.8 (beta)

- Light mode. Marble now follows your phone's light or dark setting, and changes with it while open. Light is warm paper with the same teal; dark is as before. Every screen follows, including Discover, profiles, Your account, and the sheet that asks before an app uses your account.
- The app icon has a light ground, like the phone's own icons, so it no longer looks like a black ring. With themed icons turned on, Marble's icon takes the theme colours too.
- Discover keeps strangers' posts about wanting to die or hurt themselves out of the feed, catching more of the ways people say it.

Checked on an emulator, not yet on a phone.
