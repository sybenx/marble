Apps you find in search now open inside Marble.

- Search for an app like noStrudel and tap it. It opens in Marble's web pane, on a chip that says Visiting.
- Keep turns the visit into a chip that stays, even after you restart Marble. ✕ closes a visit.
- A site from search can't sign in until you let it: hold its chip and turn on Can ask to sign in.
- Signing in from the pane now works like other site sign-ins: a new account just for that site, or Use my main account.
- Hold any chip for more: copy the address, refresh, open in your browser, sign out, or remove. Remove signs the site out and wipes what it saved on this phone.
- Android apps from search still open in your browser.
