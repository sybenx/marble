Marble 0.16.14

- Type any web address in search and tap **In Marble** to open it in Marble's web pane, then **Keep** it as a chip. Before, only apps in Marble's app list could be kept. A site you keep still asks before every sign-in.
- Devices join your account with the name you gave them in Android's settings, or their maker and model, instead of a bare model number. A device still showing its old model number renames itself the next time Marble opens.
