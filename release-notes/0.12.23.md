Marble 0.12.23 (beta)

- **Relays as feeds:** tap "Relays ›" at the top of Following.
  - Open any relay by its address and read it like a channel: what it says about itself, then its posts, newest first.
  - ★ keeps a relay in your favourites. "Add to a set" groups relays to read together. Both are the same lists Jumble uses, so they follow you there.
  - "Your people post to" lists relays the people you follow use, beyond the usual ones.
  - Share a relay as a marble.syb.lol/relay/… link. It opens in Marble, or in a browser.
