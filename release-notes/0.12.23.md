Marble 0.12.23 (beta)

- **Relays as feeds:** tap "Relays ›" at the top of Following.
  - Open any relay by its address and read it like a channel: what it says about itself, then its posts, newest first.
  - ★ keeps a relay in your favourites. "Add to a set" groups relays to read together. Both are the same lists Jumble uses, so they follow you there.
  - "Your people post to" lists relays the people you follow use, beyond the usual ones.
  - Share a relay as a marble.syb.lol/relay/… link. It opens in Marble, or in a browser.
- **Reactions, reworked** (the best of Facebook, Wisp and Jumble):
  - tap ☺+ for a small tray: your recent reactions, then 👍 ❤️ 😂 😢 👀 🧐, then + for every emoji;
  - or press and slide to one, then let go;
  - once you've reacted, the button shows yours; tap it to take it back, or hold it to change it.
  - The full picker has search, your recent ones, your own custom emoji, and every emoji by kind.
  - Your recent reactions come from what you've reacted with, so your phone, tablet and the web show the same.
  - An opened post shows each reaction and how many gave it. Tap one to give the same; hold one to see who reacted.
  - Lists of posts show no counts at all, only your own reaction.
