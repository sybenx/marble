Signing in to a site through your account page now works the way you choose. When you pick "Use a separate account for this site" there, Marble signs that site in with a new account of its own, and remembers the choice for next time.

Marble keeps your account's sign-in page published in the new place sites look for it (kind 10046), and keeps the old one in place while sites move over.

Your list of signed-in sites says more about each one: which browser it came from, how it came in, and which device let it in.

Marble Web signs in with your name: type it, and Marble sends you to your own sign-in page.
