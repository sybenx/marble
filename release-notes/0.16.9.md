Marble 0.16.9

- When an app or a site wants to change your relay list or your DM relays, Marble asks you first, every time. That includes adding a relay.
- That question offers to give the app or site its own account. Your main relay lists stay yours, and any changes it made to them are put back. Messages to your main account won't reach it, so it gets no spam meant for you.
- You can still let it change your main lists, or say Don't. Don't keeps it signed in.
- After it gets its own account, sign out in that app and sign in again with Marble to start using it.
