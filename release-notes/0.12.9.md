Marble 0.12.9 (beta)

- Discover is calmer. At the top, "Today's stories": a few headlines from news sites and blogs on the topics you like, one per topic. Tap one to read it; if people are talking about it on Hacker News, Stacker News or Lobsters, a line under it opens that discussion. "More stories" lists the rest of today's.
- Below that, posts from people, from the last two days, on your topics, from people your follows trust. Every post says why it's there ("Bitcoin · liked by 4 people you follow"). Links and articles show as preview cards. News never goes between the posts, and the feed ends instead of scrolling forever.
- Every card has a heart, reply and Save (Save goes to your bookmarks, which other apps show too). The ⋯ menu lets you follow the topic, see more or less like it, or hide that person or site.
- Fewer posts end up under the wrong topic: a post needs to really be about something to be labelled with it.
- Opening Marble no longer brings back a code you scanned earlier (after the 0.12.8 update, some saw "This account already has as many servers as it needs"). A red error now goes away by itself after a few seconds.
- Your account no longer lists Marble itself among the apps and sites you're signed in to, so it can't be removed there by mistake.

Checked on an emulator, viewing the feed as the user's own account in read-only mode; not yet on a phone.
