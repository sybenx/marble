Marble 0.12.21 (beta)

- **Only your circle (on by default):** your circle is the people you follow, plus anyone at least two of them follow.
  - Posts from outside it stay out of Following.
  - In search they still come up, after your circle's, under "More posts".
  - Under your own posts, replies from outside it fold into one "More replies" line.
  - Marking someone "Not spam" brings them in.
  - It's all worked out on this phone, and nothing is scored or shown as a number. Turn it off in Account › Following.
- **Reactions, as in Jumble:**
  - tap ☺+ under a post to pick any emoji (your recent ones first), or hold it for the quick row;
  - your own reaction shows on the button, with how many reacted;
  - an opened post shows each emoji people used and how many; tap one to add yours.
  - Replies and reposts show their counts too.
- **Translated as you read:** a post in a language you don't read is translated on this phone. "Translated from Spanish · Show original" sits at the top of the post, so a long one makes sense from its first line.
  - The first time, a language is one tap to download ("Translate from Spanish").
  - When the language isn't clear, you choose it, and Marble remembers your choice for that person.
  - Choose the languages you read in Account.
- **Pinned posts:** pinned posts come first on a profile. Pin one of your own from its ⋯ menu.
- **Polls:** vote in polls from other apps, and see how everyone voted.
- **Videos play once:** at the end a video stays on its last frame with a Replay button, instead of starting over.

Size: the app is about 31 MB (it was 16 MB), mostly for translating on the phone. It now installs on phones and tablets only, not on emulators.
