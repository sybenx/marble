Marble 0.12.21 (beta)

- **Only your circle (on by default):** your circle is the people you follow, plus anyone at least two of them follow.
  - Posts from outside it stay out of Following.
  - In search they still come up, after your circle's, under "More posts".
  - Under your own posts, replies from outside it fold into one "More replies" line.
  - Marking someone "Not spam" brings them in.
  - It's all worked out on this phone, and nothing is scored or shown as a number. Turn it off in Account › Following.
- **Videos play once:** at the end a video stays on its last frame with a Replay button, instead of starting over.
