Marble 0.12.22 (beta)

- **Articles show as cards:** a post that points to an article now shows it as a card, with its picture (if it has one), its title, a line about it, who wrote it, and how long it takes to read. Tap to read it.
- **Link previews:** a link on its own line, or at the end of a post, shows a preview with the site's name and picture. One per post, so posts don't fill up with cards.
- **Long lists of people at the end of a post** fold into one line: the first four, then "+6". Tap to see the rest.
- **The reader:**
  - draws tables, which scroll sideways while the first column stays in place;
  - shows the whole article;
  - doesn't repeat a title the article starts with;
  - says how long it takes to read.
- **Links to posts on njump, Primal, nostr.at and similar sites** open the post inside Marble.
- **No zap counts** under posts for now.
