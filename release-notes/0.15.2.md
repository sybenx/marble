Marble only publishes your account's sign-in page when your box's code names it, and no longer guesses it from the box's address, which a lookalike address passing your box through could fake.

Opening Marble goes straight to your home screen on a plain background: no sign-in screen flashing past, and no marble on the splash.

Apps that read your messages, like Postilla, no longer open Marble's signer over and over for messages it can't decrypt (“Couldn't do that: bad_message: mac”). A message that isn't to your account, or is damaged, is now turned down quietly.
- Marble Web can open your notes vault again: with Read your private lists on, a site may open your own vaults' keys, which are sealed to you with a one-time key. Anything else sealed that way still needs Read and send DMs.
