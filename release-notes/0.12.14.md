Marble 0.12.14 (beta)

- Marble's colour is now cobalt, to match the new icon: links, buttons, topic labels and what you've liked or saved. It's a little lighter in dark mode so it's easy to read.
- The browser extension and marble's website are cobalt too, with the new marble as their icon. In Brave or Chrome, reload Marble at brave://extensions (or chrome://extensions) to see it.
- A newsletter that was already on your screen before 0.12.13 no longer comes back when you open Marble again.

Checked on an emulator, not yet on a phone.
