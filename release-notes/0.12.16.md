Marble 0.12.16 (beta)

- A marble of the day. Four marbles take turns by the date, changing at midnight UTC everywhere, and from 27 October a new one arrives each night. Tap it to see it big: the first look of the day collects it, with a small flourish.
- Pull the top of the main page down and the marble grows with you; pull far enough to refresh.
- The same bar at the top of every screen: the swirl takes you home, then search, a pencil to write a post, the bell and you.
- Write a post, or share a news story with your comment: hold a story, or tap its ⋯, and choose "Post with a comment".
- Come back to Marble after five minutes or more and you're on the main page, at the top, with what's new since you last looked first. A shorter hop, to read an article or answer a message, keeps you where you were.
- Threads find more of their replies and likes, and show "12 replies · 3 reposts · 68 likes" under the post. A pasted link to a post shows the post with who quoted it, its replies and where it's mentioned; a link to a person shows posts mentioning them.
- A profile from Mastodon or Bluesky that can't be found now says so, with Try again, instead of looking empty.
- Search: type a site's name or address to go straight there.
- Your devices: hold one to rename it, and the name shows on all your devices. Each device now names itself readably instead of with a model number.
- Sites you're signed in to: tap the address to open the site.
- Today's stories no longer run out when they can't be fetched for a while: More stories shows the last week's, and says when it couldn't get the newest.
- The big marble stays centred as you tilt the phone.

Checked on an emulator, not yet on a phone.
