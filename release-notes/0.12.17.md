Marble 0.12.17 (beta)

- A security fix. Any one of your account's servers could ask your phone to help make a share of your key, without your phone checking that you had asked for one, without your lock, and without showing anything. With your phone's help that server could have made itself a device of your account and signed as you. Your phone now helps only with something you asked for (a device you scanned and approved, a server you added, a paper backup, or growing its own shares) and refuses anything else, saying which server asked. Update soon.
- Your phone no longer hands out pieces of a decryption to anyone who asks, your servers included. Reading your messages stays on your own devices.
- A refresh started on another of your devices is held until your servers have committed it, so a half-done change can't leave your devices out of step. The browser extension does the same.
- One server saying an app was removed is no longer taken as your account's answer. Conversation keys stay ready while your phone is awake, so messages open faster.
- Pulling down at the top of home grows the marble to a third of the screen at most. It slows as it gets there, stays centred and no longer runs off the screen. A refresh takes the same pull as before.

Checked with Cosign's tests (unit, and end to end: guards, paper backup, a lost device, refresh, adding a tablet), not yet on a phone.
