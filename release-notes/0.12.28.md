Marble 0.12.28 (beta)

- **Notes sync is safer:** a summary (checkpoint) is written only after reading the one there, the old single notes list is never written over a newer kind, new note keys are used only once most of your relays have them, a notes group that splits reads its new parts before writing them, and a pull that missed a page reads from as far back next time.

- **Reader:** in a day of someone's posts, tapping a post opens that post (before, the row's Open in… could open their first one). Hold a post for Open in….
- **Safer syncing between accounts and devices:** a notes sync that's interrupted keeps every unsent change, and one that stopped before saving sends it after a restart; a phone that's behind reads every notes group before writing, so another device's large import is never written over; a split notes group keeps its marker; news sources can't mix between accounts even when you switch mid-sync; news and device names count as saved only once most of your relays take them.
- The day's older note in the box changes the next day. With the older feed chosen in Advanced, the word on home says Following.
