Marble 0.12.19 (beta)

- **Three panes, a swipe apart:** Following on the left, Search in the middle (where Marble opens), and Messages on the right (empty for now, ready for when messages come).
- **Following, in three short parts:**
  - your Special Follow people;
  - Today's headlines from your topics;
  - everyone you follow, newest first, with a few posts from beyond your people that people you follow liked or shared, each saying who.
  - Each part shows a few rows; More opens the whole list. Nothing is ranked by popularity.
  - This version switches Following to this layout once. Account › Advanced lets you go back to an earlier one any time.
- **Posts:**
  - like with a heart, or hold it for 👍 ❤️ 😂 😢 👀 🧐;
  - repost, or hold to quote;
  - share, and keep in your notes, on every post and inside an opened one.
  - No counts in Following.
- **Notes on home again:** your latest three or four notes sit just above the Notes button. Tap one to search it or open its page; tick the circle when it's done.
- **Notes sync more reliably between your devices:**
  - each note syncs on its own, so a tick on one device and an edit on another both stay;
  - if two devices change the same note's text, you keep both versions;
  - a removed note stays removed everywhere;
  - a new device can catch up straight from another of yours that has Marble open.
  - Changes go out within three minutes, and when you leave Marble.
  - The first time this version runs, it moves your notes over in the background (about a minute). Nothing is lost, and older Marbles still see your notes.
- **Search as you type:** people, posts and articles come up as you type. Your own past searches and notes come first. Enter keeps the search in Searches (30 days, this phone only).
  - Name someone you follow in a search ("thailand anarko", even "thailand arko") and their posts about it come first.
- **Articles open in a reader:** set in a book typeface, with headings, quotes and pictures.
- **Quoted posts and articles show as cards.** Something made in another app shows what it is, and Open takes you to that app.
- **Links to posts open in Marble,** saved posts included, instead of the browser.
- **Videos play as you scroll**, muted until you turn the sound on, one at a time. They have controls, full screen, and save to the phone.
  - GIFs move.
  - Every picture in a post shows, not just the first four.
  - Zoomed pictures pan with your finger.
- **Likely spam:**
  - out of search and Following;
  - if you open one anyway, it says "Looks like spam" with the reason in plain words;
  - "Not spam" takes it back on this phone;
  - never applied to people you follow.
- **Files shared as Blossom links** show and open.
- **Faster:** Following no longer holds up swiping right after it loads.

Two things to know:
- **Usage notes:** this version turns on Marble's record of how you use Following. It's kept on this phone only, nothing is sent, and you can turn it off in Account › Advanced.
- **Size:** the app is about 2 MB bigger, for the spam check.
