Marble 0.12.13 (beta)

- Coming back to Marble leaves Discover as you left it. Open a story in your browser, come back, and the same stories and posts are there in the same place, the one you opened included. Only pulling down brings new ones; the automatic refresh every half hour only adds new posts below what you're reading.
- Tap the marble at the top of Discover to see it full screen; it leans a little as you tilt your phone (not if you've turned animations off). Pull down past the top and it grows a little as Discover refreshes.
- Fewer newsletters in Discover: posts from people you don't follow that are a newsletter or a digest ("In this week's issue", "Issue #42", a weekly roundup, a list of links, a subscribe or sponsored block) stay out.
- Search Marble from your launcher: in KISS, add a search provider named Marble with the address marble://search?q=%s

Checked on an emulator, not yet on a phone.
