Marble 0.12.20 (beta)

- **Nothing found? Search the web:** when a search finds no people or posts, one tap tries it with your search engine instead.
- **Choose your news (Account › News sources):**
  - add a site you read, or its RSS address: Marble finds its feed and asks which topic it belongs to;
  - take out any of Marble's sources you don't want, and put it back later.
  - Your choices follow you to your other devices.
- **Special Follow from a profile:** on someone you follow, tap ★ Special Follow to put their posts first in Following, and tap again to take them off. Account › Special Follow lists everyone on it. It's the same list other apps such as Jumble use.
