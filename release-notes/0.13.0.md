Marble now signs with Keykin: your phone and your Keykin box each hold a piece of your key, and any two pieces sign. There are no public signing servers any more.

**Before you update: accounts on the old Cosign servers do not carry over.** Marble opens on its welcome screen after this update. If your account is on Cosign servers, take its secret key out first (Your account, Advanced, Take my account out), then after updating choose "I already have an account", paste it, and scan your box's code. Marble keeps the whole key on your phone, sealed, as your backup, until you choose to forget it.

New:
- Make an account with your box, bring in a key you already have, or ask a box for an account.
- Sign in to sites by a scanned code, nostrconnect:// or a bunker:// link, and apps on your phone (NIP-55), as before.
- Your account page (account.syb.lol): what Marble does for it runs in the background, and anything hard to undo asks Yes or No in a notification.
- Adding a device, unblocking, cycling your keys and showing your whole key need your fingerprint.
