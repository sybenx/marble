Sites you sign in to now get only what you allow.

- Signing in to a site shows three switches: Sign in and post as you, which is always on; Read your private lists; and Read and send DMs.
- Read your private lists starts on only for a site that asks for them when you sign in, like Jumble. Read and send DMs starts off.
- A site without DMs on can't read your messages or send any as you. Whatever it asks for that's off is refused, with no prompt.
- You can change the switches any time in the site's sheet in Marble, or under Your account.
- Sites you signed in to before this update keep posting as you, with private lists and DMs off. Turn them on for the sites that need them.
- Keeping a site you visited from search now lets it ask to sign in. Each sign-in still asks you first.
- Your account's sign-in page pointer is published when you join a box and repaired when you sign in, so typing your name on marble.syb.lol finds you.
