Marble 0.12.18 (beta)

- A calmer home. It's the marble, the search box ("Search, or keep a thought") just above the middle, and what you kept lately. Pulling down only stretches the marble.
- Discover, a swipe up from home. It shows what people liked this week (from fiatjaf's Pyramid), newest first, with posts from your Special Follow list (the one Jumble keeps) woven in near the top. Today's news is one row you open. Pull down to refresh. The end of the list says where it all comes from.
- Search is for typing. Enter searches the web with your engine and shows its results inside Marble; links open in your browser. Save keeps what you typed in Later. The box types like a browser's address bar (no corrections, no capitals, no space after a full stop), tapping it on a results page selects the whole search, and Back from results goes home with the box empty.
- Later, synced with Marble on your Mac. Your own words are in a typewriter face; saved pages have their site's icon and title. With nothing saved, the pages you opened are still there.
- Save, in pages Marble opens. The bookmark in the page's bar puts that page in Later, and it fills once the page is saved.
- Keep in Marble, from any app's share sheet. Add your words, and the link or text you shared is kept alongside.
- Reactions: hold the heart for an emoji (your most-used first), and tap it again to change it.
- Your marbles. Looking at today's marble keeps it, and you get its badge. Tap the chip under it to see all you've kept, and show one on your profile if you like.
- Pinch the big marble to look closer; it springs back when you let go.
