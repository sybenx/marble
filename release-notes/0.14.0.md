An experiment: a site you sign in to for the first time gets a new account of its own.

- The sign-in card's main button is now Sign in with a new account. The account is made just for that site and starts empty. Nothing from your main account goes with it.
- Smaller text under it, Use my main account, signs in as you always have.
- Marble remembers the choice for each site and does not ask again.
- Sites you were already signed in to stay on your main account. Apps on your phone are unchanged.
- A Keykin account page always uses your main account, wherever it is hosted.
- Your account lists each signed-in site as Own account or Main account.

A new account is a child of your main one: any of your devices makes the same one again from the site's address, so there is nothing new to back up.
