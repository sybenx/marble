Your notes now live in a vault: encrypted on your phone, kept on your own relays and Blossom servers, the same on every device.

- The first time Marble starts after this update, it makes your notes vault and brings in the notes you already had, once. The old copies on your relays are left as they were.
- Edits go out about half a minute after you stop typing, and at once when you leave Marble.
- A note changed on two devices at once keeps both. It shows 2 versions: tap it to see them side by side, then Keep this, Keep both, or Edit.
- Removed notes go to Trash, where Restore brings them back. Empty Trash asks Yes or No first.
- Marble Web opens the same notes, however you sign in there.
- If you remove a device in Keykin and cycle the keys, your notes get a new key that device can't open.
- Changes to your lists made in another app or device now show in Recent changes. If one lost something, like your bookmarks emptied, it gets an amber dot and a notification, and Undo asks before putting the list back.
- Search results say what each one is (Web app, nsite, Android app, Website, Profile) and where a tap opens it: Marble, your browser, or Zapstore.
