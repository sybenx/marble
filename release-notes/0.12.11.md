Marble 0.12.11 (beta)

- What you've seen follows you. Posts and stories you've already seen in Discover don't come back on your other phone or tablet either. The list is kept on your account, locked so only you can read it, holds the last week, and is let go by the servers two weeks after you stop using Marble.
- A faster first open on a new phone or tablet. Instead of waiting for the whole feed to be put together (about 30 seconds in tests), Marble shows a few posts first, from people you follow and what's popular on your topics, and fills in the rest below without moving what you're reading. In good test runs the first posts came in about 5 to 10 seconds; a real phone will tell.

Checked on an emulator, not yet on a phone.
