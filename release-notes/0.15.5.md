When you move to a new box, Marble updates where sites send you to sign in. If your sign-in page is one this phone never used, it asks you once before changing it.

When your account page asks your phone for something, like a bunker link, and it fails once, your phone tries again instead of leaving the page waiting.

Your account page can ask your phone to make it your sign-in page, so you can fix it after signing in with a password.
