Signing in on a new device is one step now. Press Sign in, and it shows one code. Scan it with a phone that is already in your account and press Add. The new device goes straight to your home screen.

No other device at hand? Sign in, then Use my name: type your name, press Add on your account page, and you come back to Marble signed in.

A code you paste or open as a link shows five digits on both devices to compare first. A code you scan with Marble's own camera does not.
