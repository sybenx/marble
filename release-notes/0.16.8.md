Marble 0.16.8

- Comments show up in search, on profiles and in your notifications. Before, a comment never came back, even right after you posted it.
- A comment you open from search shows in its thread, at the comment, not at the top.
- Replying to a comment makes a comment, so the other apps in that thread see it there.
- A comment on a web page or an article says what it's on.
