Marble 0.16.18

- Amethyst and other apps that sign with Marble no longer pop up "Couldn't do that" or open Marble over and over. Private zaps sent to you are opened quietly, and when an app asks many things at once, Marble answers them together.
- Marble no longer shows error codes. When something goes wrong it says so in plain words, and keeps the details on your phone for troubleshooting.
