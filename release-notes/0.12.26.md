Marble 0.12.26 (beta)

- **Approvals:** everything waiting for your key now has its own screen: requests to sign, a deletion you can still undo, recovery, a device joining, and your paper backup. "More waiting for you" on home opens it, and the bell points to it.
- **Safer list changes:** following, muting, Special Follow, favourite relays, relay sets and pins each start from your newest list anywhere, change only the one entry, and are checked again before they're signed. If your current list can't be found, Marble refuses rather than starting a new one.
- **A four-week try-out in Notes:**
  - For the first two weeks nothing changes.
  - After that, notes you haven't looked at in a while sit lower in Notes. They never leave it.
  - Hold an older note that comes up in search to choose Next step, Keep in view or Let go (Let go marks it done). Kept-in-view notes sit at the top of Notes.
  - At the end, Marble asks you once whether Notes feels calmer.
  - What you look at stays on this phone; only what you decide syncs.
  - After the four weeks, Notes stops showing how many notes you have and how old each one is.
