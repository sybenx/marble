Marble 0.16.11

- Translate from Spanish (or any language) now works. Before, it downloaded the language and then showed nothing.
- On a site in the web pane, a page that is one post or one person, like a post on Jumble, shows Open in Marble. It opens the post in Marble, translated if it needs to be. Back takes you to the page.
- A nostr: link tapped anywhere on your phone opens in Marble. If Marble can't read the link, it says so.
- Sharing just a post's link or a nostr: address to Marble opens the post instead of saving it.
