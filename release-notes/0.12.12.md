Marble 0.12.12 (beta)

- Going back keeps your place. Open a post from someone's profile, from search or from More stories, go back, and you're where you were instead of at the top.
- Tap the time on a post ("6h") to copy its ID, so you can find the same post in another app. The ⋯ menu also has "Copy link", a web link that opens anywhere, for sharing with people who don't use Marble.
- A new app icon: the swirl as a cobalt glass marble, in the colours of the marble inside the app. Your launcher may need a restart to show it.

Checked on an emulator, not yet on a phone.
