Marble 0.12.29 (beta)

- **Notes sync:** an older Marble's changes to the single notes list are merged before it's written again; the notes summary is never written over a newer kind; a pull that missed a later page, or stopped early, reads from as far back next time.

- **Reader, as Google Reader:**
  - **Rows:** each row is a star and text, with no other icons. Read rows sit on a faint tint, and the row you have open shows a thin edge down its side.
  - **Opening:** tap a row to open it in place, and tap it again to close it. Only one is open at a time.
  - **The opened item:**
    - Its title is the way out: a story's headline opens its site, and a post's name and time open the post.
    - "from Source" under the title shows only that source.
    - The whole summary shows. A day's posts are all listed, oldest first, with nothing boxed.
  - **Under it, in words:** Star · Share with a note · Share · Keep unread · Open in… (for posts) · Discussion (when there is one).
  - **Share with a note** opens right there. It keeps the item privately in Notes unless you turn on Post publicly.
  - **Along the top:** All, Starred and your folders. A folder is bold when something new has come in since you last opened it, with no dots and no numbers. "Add" is a word.
  - **Above the list:** List · Expanded, and Mark all as read, with Undo.
  - **Earlier:** unread items come first, then a quiet "Earlier" line. Rows don't move while you read.
  - **In Expanded view,** an item counts as read once it has scrolled past the top for a second.
  - **Keep unread and Star:** Keep unread no longer keeps an item past its usual time; to keep something, star it. Un-starring removes its note, with Undo.
- Marbles and interests are saved to your relay list's write relays, and count as saved only once most of them take it.
