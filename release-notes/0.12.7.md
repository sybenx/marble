Marble 0.12.7 (beta)

- Discover is now a front page. Stories from news sites and blogs on twelve everyday topics (AI, games, food, privacy, photos and more) come in between posts. Tap one to read it on its site.
- It learns what you like from what you like and open, and puts that first. Tap the topic under a story to follow it or see less of it. ⋯ on a story: Not interested, less about its topic, or less from that site.
- Nothing shows twice. Once something has been on your screen for a moment, it doesn't come back. Pulling down always brings things you haven't seen.
- It never runs out. Past what matches you come other topics, then more finds from further back.
- It opens at once, on what you had last time, and refreshes every 30 minutes while it's open.
- What you like is worked out on your phone, never on a server. A copy is kept on your account, locked so only you can read it, so your other phones start from it too.
- Adding a server from its own code: a clear "Add this server" button. The sheet no longer shows the address it's reached through as if it were the server, and the first letters of the names are no longer cut off.

Measured on an emulator, not yet on a phone.
