Marble 0.12.7 (beta)

- Discover is new. News stories from sites across a dozen topics now sit between posts, ordered by what you like. Tap a story to read it on its site; tap its topic to follow it or see less of it. The topics you follow are under Your account.
- Nothing shows twice. Once you've looked at something, it's gone; pull down and you get things you haven't seen. Discover never runs out.
- What you like is learned on your phone and nowhere else, and saved to your account encrypted so only you can read it. A new phone picks it up without learning again.
- Opening is as quick as before: last time's page shows at once, and learning happens afterwards.

Checked on an emulator, not yet on a phone.
