Marble 0.12.27 (beta)

- **A quieter home:**
  - At rest, home shows only the mark, a Search box, the word Reader top left and your picture top right.
  - There are no tabs, no Notes button, no recent notes under the box, and no bell.
  - Tap Reader, or swipe, to go to Reader. On Reader, the mark takes you back to Search.
  - Your picture opens your profile, Notes and searches, Approvals, Devices and backup, Settings, Switch account, Your marbles and About Marble.
  - A card shows on home only while something waits for you, such as an approval.
- **The Search box:**
  - Tap it to see your two latest searches or notes, then Notes and searches. From the notes try-out's third week, it also shows what you keep in view and one older note to decide about.
  - Type a place's name, such as "reader" or "settings", to go there.
- **No more counts or ages:** Notes no longer shows how many notes you have or how old each one is.
- **Reader replaces Following:** Reader is a plain list with one row per story.
  - **Rows:**
    - Each row shows the source, the title (bold until you've read it), a line of the story and the date. Tap a row to read it in place; tap again to close it.
    - Every row has Open in…. For a post or article it offers your Nostr app, Marble or njump.me, plus the page it links to.
    - An opened story has a discussion button when it has comments somewhere, and a star that keeps it in your notes.
    - Hold a row for Keep unread, Share with a note, Share, or Mute that source.
  - **Posts:**
    - Posts look the same as everywhere else in Marble: names instead of codes, quoted posts and articles as cards, and pictures (Blossom ones too).
    - Posts and articles marked sensitive stay hidden until you tap them.
    - Links to a post on njump, Primal, marble.syb.lol and similar sites open in Marble, and Back takes you to the same place in Reader.
  - **Folders:**
    - Folders run along the top: Read first (your Special Follow), Your people, News, Writers and Relays, plus your own. A dot shows a folder has something new. There are no counts.
    - A folder's ⋯ has Mark all as read, and List or Expanded view. In Expanded view, a story counts as read once it has been fully on screen for a second.
  - **People's posts:** short posts from one day are collected into one row, "Jo · 6 posts". Articles, links, pictures, videos and quotes get rows of their own. Replies don't show.
  - **Read state:**
    - What you've read, and what you kept unread, stays that way on your other devices.
    - Marble only updates your account's copy after most of your relays have answered, so a slow relay can't overwrite it.
    - A day's posts from someone stay bold until you've seen every one of them.
    - Read stories leave Reader after a day for news, two days for people and a week for articles. Unread ones stay up to 30 days, and starred ones stay. Stories you never opened are never marked read.
  - **Sources:**
    - Tap + to add a site or a person, turn Your people on or off, and mute or unsubscribe from a source. Muting takes effect at once.
    - Your news sites have moved into a News folder. Who you read stays private.
- **Share with a note:** the note stays in your notes and nothing is posted.
  - To post, turn on Post publicly; it's off every time you open it. You then get a separate box for what everyone will see, and five seconds to undo.
  - An article is shared as a quote.
- The old Following feed is still in Advanced if you want it back.
