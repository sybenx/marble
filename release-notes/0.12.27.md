Marble 0.12.27 (beta)

- **Reader replaces Following:** the middle tab is now Reader, a plain list with one row a story.
  - Each row shows the source, the title (bold until you've read it), a line of the story and the date. Tap a row to read it in place; tap again to close it.
  - Opened, a story has Open in…, a discussion button when the story has comments somewhere, and a star that keeps it in your notes.
  - Hold a row for Keep unread, Share with a note or Share.
  - Folders run along the top: Read first (your Special Follow), Your people, News, Writers and Relays, plus your own. A dot shows a folder has something new. There are no counts.
  - A folder's ⋯ has Mark all as read, and List or Expanded view. In Expanded view a story counts as read once you've scrolled past it.
  - People's short posts from one day are collected into one row, "Jo · 6 posts". Their articles, links, pictures and videos get rows of their own. Replies don't show.
  - Read stories leave Reader after a day for news, two days for people and a week for articles. Unread ones stay up to 30 days, and starred ones stay.
  - Tap + to add a site or a person, turn Your people on or off, and mute or unsubscribe from a source. Your news sites have moved into a News folder. Who you read stays private.
- **Share with a note:** the note stays in your notes and nothing is posted. To post, turn on Post publicly (it's off every time you open it). You then get a separate box for what everyone will see, and five seconds to undo.
- The old Following feed is still in Advanced if you want it back.
