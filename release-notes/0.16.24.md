Marble 0.16.24

- Notes you write on your phone reach your other devices again, even if you've signed out of the web many times. A phone that got stuck sends its waiting notes on its next sync.
- Search: your archive only shows posts with every word you typed, instead of filling the top of every search.
- `from:name words` finds that person's posts that have the words, and nothing else, even if you don't follow them. Marble picks the real account, not one of its copycats. `from:name` on its own shows their latest posts.
- Posts in search always have the words you typed.
