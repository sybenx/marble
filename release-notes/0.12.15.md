Marble 0.12.15 (beta)

- Today's stories are back at the top. Scrolling past a story no longer counts as having seen it: only stories you open (or say you're not interested in) go away. Stories you keep scrolling past make way for newer ones after a few days, and the box at the top is never empty while there's news to show.
- Newsletters from people you don't follow are back in Discover, just lower down. If you like one, open its link or tap "More like this", they stop being pushed down for you; "Not interested" pushes them further. Posts about wanting to die or hurt oneself from strangers still never show.
